# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our software. By using our software, you agree to the terms of this privacy policy.
The application requires a download onto your device, then runs entirely locally in your browser.

## Application Download and Update

When you download or update the application, we may collect certain information about your device and operating system.
This information is collected and managed by CloudFlare through its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
We may use it to improve the application and gather analytics about how the application is used. We do not share this information with third parties.

## Data Collected

We do not collect any data you enter into the application. Data is stored locally on your device but may be backed up or synced to cloud storage providers.
Your connection to these providers is managed by the provider and is subject to their privacy policies. We do not have access to your data stored in these providers.

We may collect general information on how the application is used, such as the number of times the application is opened and the features that are used.
This is governed by the CloudFlare privacy policy linked above as well as the Umami analytics privacy policy at [https://umami.is/privacy](https://umami.is/privacy).
We may collect broad information about your device and operating system, such as the type of device, operating system version, and browser type.
We may collect information about errors that occur while using the application, such as error messages and stack traces. This information is used to improve the application and fix bugs.

We do not disclose or sell any personal information to third parties other than those listed above or required by law.
